# It's been a little over a year since their passing

I miss y'all, Sutrebor and Chunkey, good friends from Windfola days.

<!--more-->

## Sutrebor

It was not long after joining Honor and Glory on Windfola, that I met
Sutrebor for the first time. Sut was one of our stalwart Guardians. We played
together in many endgame raids and instances from Moria on through the
disbanding of the Kinship.

## Chunkey

I didn't know Chunkey by that name when I met him through House of Batiatus.
He mostly played as his rune-keeper and didn't speak much in voice chat. A
very different version of Chunkey than when I met him again through his own
kinship, The Ancients on Arkenstone.
